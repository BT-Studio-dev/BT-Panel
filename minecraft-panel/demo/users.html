Access model

Administrators

Full access: every server, users, settings, activity log and the installer.

Members

See and control only the servers they created. They can start, stop, edit files and take backups of their own servers.

Sessions

Opaque tokens stored hashed in SQLite. Changing a password revokes every session for that user.
